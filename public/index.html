About Me

I’m Noibedya, a product designer. I work on making complicated things easier to use. Not just cleaner. Actually easier.

I start with research. I want to know what people are trying to do, where they get stuck, and why, before I open Figma. I prototype fast and change my mind a lot.

I’ve worked at startups and bigger companies, mostly in fintech, SaaS, and consumer apps. I’m comfortable leading design end to end or working closely with engineers and PMs to ship something real.

Outside work, I read about design history and cognitive psychology. And I’m always looking for better coffee.
